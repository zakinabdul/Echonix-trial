Got Questions?

Clear Your Doubts

The cost depends on your system size. A typical 3KW residential system costs ₹1.5–2 lakh before subsidy. With the PM Surya Ghar subsidy, you can receive up to ₹78,000, significantly reducing your investment.

Under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, homeowners can get ₹30,000 for 1KW, ₹60,000 for 2KW, and ₹78,000 for 3KW and above systems.

A typical residential installation is completed in 2–4 days after all approvals are in place. We handle KSEB and government approvals on your behalf.

On-grid systems do not work during power cuts for safety reasons. However, our Hybrid systems with battery backup provide uninterrupted power even when the grid is down.

Solar panels require minimal maintenance — primarily cleaning every 3–6 months. We offer Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) plans for worry-free ownership.

Most residential solar systems in Kerala achieve payback within 4–6 years, after which you enjoy virtually free electricity for 20+ years.
